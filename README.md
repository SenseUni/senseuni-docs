# SenseUni Labs

**Building intelligent retail systems through Vision AI, IoT, edge computing, and hardware engineering.**

SenseUni Labs develops integrated smart retail solutions that combine artificial intelligence, connected devices, cloud software, and commercial refrigeration engineering.

Our work brings together software, hardware, and real-world retail applications to help build smarter, more connected retail experiences.

## Technology Areas

- **Vision AI** — AI-powered visual recognition and intelligent retail applications.
- **IoT & Edge Computing** — Connected devices, edge systems, and real-time data processing.
- **Cloud Software** — Software platforms and services for connected retail systems.
- **Hardware Engineering** — Product design, system integration, and engineering for physical retail environments.
- **Smart Retail** — Integrated solutions connecting AI, devices, software, and commercial refrigeration.

## Our Products

- **YEPZI Smart Coolers** — Smart cooler solutions for connected retail environments.
- **SenseUni AI Kit** — AI technology and integration capabilities for smart retail applications.
